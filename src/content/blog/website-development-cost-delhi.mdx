One of the first questions any Delhi business owner asks before commissioning a website is: what is this going to cost me?

It's a fair question, and it deserves a straight answer — not a vague "it depends" that leaves you more confused than when you started.

This is a breakdown of real costs based on what we charge and what you'd typically pay other agencies in Delhi. The numbers reflect work done in 2024–25.

---

## Why does the price vary so much?

Before the numbers, a quick explanation of why two agencies can quote ₹20,000 and ₹2,00,000 for what sounds like the same thing.

The difference usually comes down to:

- **Who builds it** — a freelancer using a theme, a mid-size agency with a team, or a development studio building custom
- **What it's built on** — WordPress with a template vs. a custom-coded site vs. a full web application
- **Complexity** — number of pages, features, integrations, backend work
- **Design** — a template with your colours changed vs. a design created from scratch for your brand

All of these affect the price significantly. A ₹15,000 website and a ₹1,50,000 website are genuinely different products — not the same thing at different margins.

---

## Basic business website

**What this is:** 5–8 pages. Home, About, Services, Gallery or Portfolio, Contact. A contact form. Mobile-responsive. Clean design.

**Who needs this:** Local businesses — a salon, a clinic, a restaurant, a coaching centre, a logistics company, a real estate broker. Something that looks professional and tells visitors what you do and how to reach you.

**Typical cost in Delhi: ₹25,000 – ₹60,000**

At the lower end (₹25,000–₹35,000), you're usually getting a WordPress site with a premium theme adapted to your brand. Fast to build, looks decent, does the job.

At the higher end (₹45,000–₹60,000), you're getting a custom-designed site built specifically for your business — not a template. Better performance, easier to maintain, no plugin conflicts.

**Timeline:** 2–3 weeks.

## Business website with advanced features

**What this is:** Everything in the basic website, plus things like:

- A blog or news section
- WhatsApp and call integration
- Google Maps embed
- Photo/video gallery
- Online enquiry tracking
- Basic SEO setup

**Who needs this:** A business that wants to generate leads through the website, not just provide contact details.

**Typical cost: ₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000**

At this level, the site is being built to actually bring in enquiries — not just exist on the internet. Design, loading speed, mobile experience, and SEO all matter here.

---

## E-commerce website

**What this is:** A product catalogue, shopping cart, payment gateway integration (Razorpay, PayU, or Paytm), order management, delivery tracking.

**Who needs this:** A shop or brand selling products online.

**Typical cost: ₹80,000 – ₹3,00,000**

A basic WooCommerce store for 50–100 products starts around ₹80,000. A custom-built e-commerce platform with inventory management, multiple payment methods, and a branded customer experience is in the ₹2–3 lakh range.

Be cautious of anything under ₹40,000 for e-commerce — what you usually get is a template install that hasn't been properly configured for speed, security, or your specific product catalogue.

---

## Custom web application

**What this is:** A website with a backend — user accounts, admin dashboards, bookings, payments, data management. Something that actually does work, not just displays information.

Examples: a booking system, a student management platform, an inventory app, a client portal, a SaaS product.

**Typical cost: ₹1,50,000 – ₹8,00,000+**

This varies enormously based on what the application needs to do. A basic booking system for a clinic is different from a multi-user platform with billing and reporting.

A rough starting point: if your application has user login, a database, and admin functionality, expect to start from ₹1.5 lakh. Most serious web applications are in the ₹3–6 lakh range.

## What about really cheap quotes?

You'll find people in Delhi offering websites for ₹5,000–₹15,000. Some of these are fine for very basic use cases — a one-page presence so you can share a link on a business card.

The problem comes when:

- The site is slow (hurts your Google ranking and user experience)
- It's not mobile-friendly (most Indian users browse on phones)
- It can't be updated without the developer
- There's no SEO setup at all
- The design uses a generic template that looks like 50 other sites

We've rebuilt several such sites for Delhi businesses who got a cheap site built and then realised after six months that it wasn't doing anything for them. That's money spent twice.

## Ongoing costs to factor in

Beyond the initial build, a website has running costs:

- **Domain name:** ₹800–₹1,500/year
- **Hosting:** ₹3,000–₹15,000/year depending on traffic and requirements
- **Maintenance:** Optional retainer, typically ₹3,000–₹8,000/month for updates, backups, security monitoring
- **SEO:** Separate from development — this is ongoing work to help your site rank on Google

## How to evaluate any quote you receive

When you get a quote from any agency or freelancer, ask these questions:

1. **Is this a custom design or a template?** Know which you're getting.
2. **What platform is it built on?** WordPress, custom, or something else — and can you update it yourself?
3. **What's included for SEO?** Basic on-page SEO should be included. Ongoing SEO is separate.
4. **Who owns the files?** The website code and design should be yours when the project is done.
5. **What happens after launch?** Is there a support period? What does ongoing maintenance cost?

A higher quote isn't always better, and a lower quote isn't always worse. The right question is whether the person quoting can actually deliver what they're promising.
